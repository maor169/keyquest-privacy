Zombie: Last Rescue: Support

Rescue survivors across eight infected cities, escort them to the helicopter and get out alive.

Get help

Report a bug or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/maor169/keyquest-privacy/issues. Please include your device model and the stage you were playing.

Common questions

Co-op partner not found on Wi-Fi? Both devices must be on the same network and allow Local Network access in Settings → Privacy → Local Network.

Progress after reinstalling? Progress is stored on the device, so a reinstall starts a new campaign.
