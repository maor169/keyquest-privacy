Zombie: Last Rescue: Privacy Policy

KeyQuest Studio · Effective 13 November 2026

Summary

Zombie: Last Rescue does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. There are no ads, no third-party tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stage stars, unlocked weapons, settings such as music and blood effects) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online co-op (optional)

If you choose to play online co-op, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay, provided by Unity Technologies) to connect you with your partner. An anonymous, randomly generated player ID is created for the session, and your network address is processed by Unity's servers only to relay game traffic. No account or personal details are required. See Unity's privacy policy.

Nearby Wi-Fi co-op (optional)

Local co-op finds a partner on the same Wi-Fi network using Bonjour. This traffic stays on your local network. The game asks for Local Network permission only when you open co-op.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions or support, open an issue at github.com/maor169/keyquest-privacy/issues or see the support page.

מדיניות פרטיות (עברית)

המשחק לא אוסף שם, אימייל, מיקום או כל מידע אישי אחר. אין פרסומות, אין מעקב ואין רכישות בתוך המשחק. ההתקדמות נשמרת רק במכשיר שלך. במשחק משותף אונליין (לבחירתך) נעשה שימוש בשירותי Unity עם מזהה אנונימי זמני כדי לחבר בין השחקנים. משחק משותף ב-Wi-Fi נשאר ברשת המקומית.
